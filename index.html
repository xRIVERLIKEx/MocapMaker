Tips for a clean capture
  • Stand 2–3 meters from the camera, facing it, with your whole body in the picture.
  • Bright, even light and a plain background help the most. Baggy clothes can hide your elbows and knees.
  • Minecraft arms and legs have no elbows or knees, so a bent arm becomes one straight arm pointing at your hand.
  • Act in place. Walking, jumping and turning all the way around are not recorded.
  • For kicks and knee-ups, turning about 45° from the camera lets it see how high your leg goes.
  • Start and end with your arms relaxed at your sides so the move blends in smoothly.
